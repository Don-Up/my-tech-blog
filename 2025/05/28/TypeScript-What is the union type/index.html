What is the union type

Foreword

in TypeScript, Union Types are an important tool for enhancing type flexibility and expressive power. It allows a variable or parameter to be any of a variety of types, thus better describing a variety of data and business scenarios in the real world. Understanding the definition, usage, and type narrowing mechanisms of union types is critical to writing robust and flexible code. This article will elaborate on the basic concepts, usage, and typical examples of union types to help developers better grasp this powerful feature.
